# Disclaimer & acceptable use

cobweb is a **self-hosted** tool. It automates a headless browser and an
HTTP client so that an operator can retrieve media from web pages **they are
authorised to access** — for example, resolving the playable stream URL behind
a player embed on a service the operator has an account with, so it can be
handed to a media player.

## Acceptable use

Run cobweb only against services where **you have the right to access the
content** and where doing so does not violate that service's terms. cobweb is
intended for personal, self-hosted media setups.

## Not acceptable

- Circumventing technical protection measures to obtain content you are not
  entitled to.
- Redistributing or publicly re-streaming content you do not own the rights to.
- Bypassing access controls, rate limits, or bot mitigation for abusive,
  high-volume, or commercial scraping.
- Any use that infringes copyright or breaches a provider's terms of service in
  your jurisdiction.

The project ships **no content sources, no site-specific scrapers, and no lists
of services.** What you point it at, and whether that is lawful where you are,
is your responsibility.
